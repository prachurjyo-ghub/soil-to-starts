SOIL TO STARS — ENGLISH / BANGLA FRONTEND PROTOTYPE

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract this ZIP completely.
2. Open Soil_to_Stars/index.html in a modern browser.
3. Use “Open workspace” to explore the field-planning tools.

Included: homepage, workspace, farmland video, images, Bengali fonts,
English/Bangla language switch, light/dark themes, and branch cursor control.
No installation, account, or backend is required.

The leaf button beside the theme control switches the branch cursor on/off
on desktop devices. EN / বাংলা changes the language and remembers your choice.
Field inputs and saved plans stay in the browser; they do not sync between devices.

This is a frontend demonstration. Crop scores and environmental data are
illustrative; live NASA data and validated planting advice are not connected.
