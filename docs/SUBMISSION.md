## Competition rules, rights and submission

Read the full [official participant terms](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/legal/) before release; the indexed 2026 text was checked on 28 October 2026. This checklist is not a substitute for those terms:

- Original solution content must be freely available or OSI open source; the terms provide an Apache-2.0 default when no OSI license is specified. The root Apache-2.0 license now covers original code; external asset clearance remains pending.
- Verify third-party reuse/redistribution rights and document applicable licenses. Cite every source, including free code, data and assets.
- Do not submit secrets, restricted material or private personal information. Recognizable names, voices and likenesses of under-18s should not appear in submissions; obtain required consent for adults in media.
- Do not imply NASA endorsement. Keep participation and branding claims accurate.

## Demonstration gate

Name the exact NASA product actually used, show its manifest and one reproducible computation, explain the three-step decision and a rejected alternative, show uncertainty/incomplete behavior, and replay a verified offline case. Film actual working UI only. Do not narrate planned features as shipped. The exact portal/media requirements must be confirmed before final exports.
